## How I Deploy AI

- **Go deep on the operation:** map workflows, data, stakeholders, incentives,
  and the context behind the problem before proposing a solution.
- **Turn context into strategy:** identify and prioritize AI use cases by
  business impact, operational feasibility, and adoption potential.
- **Own the path to production:** design, build, and integrate agentic AI
  systems with the controls, evaluation, and iteration required for real use.
- **Bridge technical and executive teams:** translate operational detail into
  decisions, plans, and business impact for everyone from end users to C-level
  stakeholders.
- **Build the product feedback loop:** bring field insight back to product and
  engineering, improving the solution through real-world usage.
- **Grow durable client value:** connect delivery quality, adoption, and
  expansion opportunities across a B2B portfolio.
